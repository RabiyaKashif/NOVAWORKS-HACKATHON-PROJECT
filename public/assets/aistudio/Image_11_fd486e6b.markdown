## Brand & Style

This design system blends high-end editorial sophistication with an AI-native cosmic precision. Built for next-generation enterprise workforce platforms, it balances executive authority, intellectual clarity, and visionary dynamism.

The aesthetic fuses **Minimalism** with **Modern Celestial Geometry**:
- An expansive, warm stone canvas accented with sweeping celestial orbit arcs, fine node markers, and constellation dot matrices.
- High-contrast, literary display serifs that convey institutional permanence alongside razor-sharp, geometric sans-serif UI typography.
- Floating, pill-contoured containers, pill-shaped primary CTAs with accent circle badges, and micro-fine hairline borders that evoke orbital mechanics and astronomical instruments.

## Colors

The palette establishes an intellectual, luminous ambiance rooted in an eggshell-stone surface illuminated by deep cosmic sapphire and electric violet accents.

- **Primary (`#0D1B3E`)**: Midnight Sapphire. Anchors high-priority actions, primary buttons, pill headers, and dominant editorial typography.
- **Secondary (`#6344E7`)**: Electric Iris Violet. Represents AI agency, constellation motifs, action badges, and orbital highlight points.
- **Tertiary (`#8B5CF6`)**: Soft Ultraviolet. Used for delicate orbital gradients, hovering states, and glowing node halos.
- **Neutral (`#F5F4F0`)**: Warm Limestone / Alabaster. The foundational background tone, preventing harsh clinical sterile whites while maintaining high typographic contrast.
- **Canvas Overlays & Outlines**: Hairline borders employ low-opacity sapphire tints (`rgba(13, 27, 62, 0.08)` to `rgba(13, 27, 62, 0.12)`) and orbital trajectories utilize ultra-thin semi-opaque lines (`rgba(13, 27, 62, 0.06)`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy creates tension between classical editorial grandeur and futuristic technological minimalism.

- **Editorial Headings (`Newsreader`)**: Reserved for primary hero statements, section narrative anchors, and high-impact value propositions. Characters display transitional high-contrast serifs with gentle fluid italics where emphasis is required.
- **Interface & Narrative Body (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Neutral, highly legible grotesque sans with open counters that ensure crisp rendering across dense enterprise dashboards and marketing narratives.
- **Pre-Title Eyebrows (`label-caps`)**: Styled strictly in uppercase with wide letter tracking (`0.2em`) using the Electric Iris hue (`#6344E7`) to evoke technical telemetry tags (e.g., `START FROM THE FUTURE`).

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial design prioritizes monumental breathing room and mathematical symmetry.

- **Grid Framework**: A 12-column responsive fluid grid anchored within a maximum width of `1280px` for marketing canvases and `1440px` for dense enterprise interfaces.
- **Rhythm & Padding**: Sections transition with generous vertical rhythm (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`). Hero stages frame centered content with dynamic vertical padding, allowing orbital SVG lines to sweep across the outer margins without visual collision.
- **Navigation Dock**: Fixed or floating pill header suspended `space-lg` from the viewport ceiling, centered horizontally with safe padding on either side.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is conveyed through translucent white floating docks, fine micro-borders, and whisper-soft diffused shadows rather than harsh stacked elevations:

- **Floating Glass Surfaces**: Elevated navigation docks and interactive modal overlays use pure white surfaces with high opacity (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)` to `0.98`) complemented by a `16px` backdrop blur.
- **Ambient Shadows**: Shadows are tinted slightly with the primary sapphire hue to mimic natural atmospheric dispersion:
  - *Floating Nav Bar / Island*: `0 10px 30px -10px rgba(13, 27, 62, 0.08), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(13, 27, 62, 0.04)`.
  - *Hero Primary CTA*: `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(13, 27, 62, 0.25)`.
- **Low-Contrast Hairlines**: All floating containers, cards, and input fields feature a 1px perimeter border rendered in `rgba(13, 27, 62, 0.07)` to guarantee edge fidelity against the alabaster background.
- **Orbital Vector Depths**: Background celestial paths use subtle `1px` stroked SVG ellipses with opacity scaled between `0.06` and `0.15`, peppered with orbiting node badges (`#6344E7`) enclosed in low-opacity lavender aura rings (`rgba(99, 68, 231, 0.12)`).

## Shapes

The geometric architecture is defined by full pill geometries (`roundedness: 3`) balanced with subtly curved structural surfaces:

- **Full Pill Components (`9999px`)**: Applied consistently to interactive triggers, primary and secondary CTA buttons, floating navigation header bars, search pills, and status tags.
- **Circular Badges (`rounded-full`)**: Embedded inside pill buttons for directional arrows and icon accents, establishing a repeating circular theme.
- **Structural Containers (`rounded-xl` to `rounded-2xl`)**: Feature cards and dashboard modules employ generous border radii (`1.5rem` to `2rem`) to harmonize with the circular orbital design language.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Hero Pill**: Deep Midnight Sapphire (`#0D1B3E`) background, crisp white typography (`label-md`), full pill curvature (`9999px`), padding `0.75rem 0.75rem 0.75rem 1.75rem`. Features an embedded circular action badge on the trailing side: a 36px Electric Violet circle (`#6344E7`) harboring a centered white directional arrow or icon.
- **Navigation Pill CTA ('Get a Demo')**: Midnight Sapphire (`#0D1B3E`), uppercase/sentence-case bold sans typography, compact pill padding `0.65rem 1.5rem`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary Action**: Transparent surface, Midnight Sapphire text, soft hover tint (`rgba(13, 27, 62, 0.04)`), pill-shaped hover state.

### Navigation Header
- **Floating Island Dock**: Horizontally centered pill container suspended over the page hero. Pure white translucent fill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)`), `backdrop-blur(16px)`, `1px` subtle border in `rgba(13, 27, 62, 0.08)`, and pill curvature (`9999px`).
- **Links**: Fluid sans typography (`#0D1B3E`) with subtle downward chevron indicators for dropdowns and smooth purple color shifts on hover.

### Eyebrow / Telemetry Badges
- Floating uppercase labels (`label-caps`) styled in Electric Iris (`#6344E7`) with wide `0.2em` letter spacing. Can be displayed standalone or encapsulated in an ultra-subtle pill badge with a 1px border.

### Cards & Modules
- Clean alabaster or pure white surfaces with `1.5rem` rounded corners, hairlines in `rgba(13, 27, 62, 0.06)`, and faint sapphire shadows.
- Can host fine orbital arc overlays and micro constellation indicators to highlight automated AI agent status.

### Form Inputs & Fields
- Rounded pill text inputs with off-white inner surfaces, hairline borders (`rgba(13, 27, 62, 0.12)`), focused states featuring an Electric Violet glow ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(99, 68, 231, 0.2)`), and sapphire placeholder text.
